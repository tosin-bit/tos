## Removed, because no source supports it

The original build prompt specified these. Nothing in the research pack supports them, so they
were taken off a site that carries a real person's name.

- **Nenneh 100** — "one hundred women a year". The only 100 figure in the sources is the UTG
  Students' Union's own target for students, explicitly noted as *not* her verified beneficiary total.
- **The Table** — a private eight-person invitation-only dinner series.
- **The Conversation** — a quarterly public interview series, and the "At Nenneh's Table" episodes.
- **Writing** — five essay titles and their body text. She has not published these.
- **Culture gallery** — ten fashion/travel images that do not exist.
- **speaking@ / partnership@ / press@ nennehcheyassin.com** — invented addresses.
- **Three-pillar Foundation structure** (Women & Wealth / Education & Opportunity / Health & Dignity)
  — no registered foundation name, registration record or programme structure was located.

Restore any of these only with real material behind them.
